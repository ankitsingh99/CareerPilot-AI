# Interview Preparation Guide for Ankit Kumar Singh

## STAR Format
Structure answers as: **Situation** (context), **Task** (your responsibility), **Action** (what you did), **Result** (outcome).
Keep answers to 1-2 minutes. Be specific. End with what you learned or the quantifiable impact.

---

## Ready-Made STAR Examples

### 1. Statistical Data Quality & Drift Model (ZS Associates)
- **Skill Demonstrated:** Data Quality, Anomaly Modeling, SQL, Problem Solving
- **Situation:** Downstream predictive models and client reporting pipelines across terabytes of healthcare data were experiencing high failure rates due to unexpected vendor data drift and distribution anomalies.
- **Task:** Build an automated, proactive data quality framework to detect anomalies before data entered production tables.
- **Action:** Engineered and deployed a statistical Data-Quality-Model in SQL to analyze incoming distribution curves, track data drift across partitions, and trigger automated alerts upon threshold breaches.
- **Result:** Slashed production data errors by 40%, significantly improved downstream ML feature reliability, and won ZS Associates' prestigious **'Super Innova'** Award.
- **Use for:** *"Tell me about a time you solved a complex data quality issue"*, *"Describe your proudest technical achievement"*, *"How do you handle data reliability?"*

### 2. Autonomous Logistics Reconciliation & Dispute Agent (Rush by Kira)
- **Skill Demonstrated:** Autonomous AI Agents, API Integration, Webhooks, Financial Impact
- **Situation:** The D2C business was suffering unseen budgetary leakage due to courier shipping overcharges and dimension discrepancies between Shopify orders and courier bills.
- **Task:** Build an automated reconciliation mechanism to detect discrepancies and recover overbilled amounts without manual auditing.
- **Action:** Architected an end-to-end multi-source reconciliation agent combining Shopify REST APIs, iThinkLogistics webhooks, and automated Gmail CSV parsing to cross-reference dimensions/weights; automated dispute email generation directly to courier partners.
- **Result:** Successfully recovered ₹20,000+ in shipping overcharges with zero ongoing manual intervention.
- **Use for:** *"How do you design autonomous agent workflows?"*, *"Tell me about an automation project with direct financial impact"*, *"How do you integrate APIs and webhooks?"*

### 3. Multi-Source Ingestion Standardization & HIPAA Governance (ZS Associates)
- **Skill Demonstrated:** Big Data Architecture, Data Governance, HIPAA Compliance, Cross-Functional Team Leadership
- **Situation:** Healthcare marketing and media data from disparate platforms (Meta, Google Ads, DSPs like DeepIntent, Lasso, PulsePoint) arrived in inconsistent schemas with sensitive healthcare professional (HCP) identifiers.
- **Task:** Lead a team of four to build a vendor-agnostic standardized data ingestion layer while enforcing strict regulatory data privacy.
- **Action:** Developed a unified ingestion framework using Snowflake Data Lake and Hive; established file specifications with external media agencies; integrated automated HCP data stripping and masking protocols into ingestion pipelines for full HIPAA compliance.
- **Result:** Eliminated vendor ingestion friction, unified cross-channel analytics, and ensured 100% HIPAA compliance.
- **Use for:** *"How do you manage data governance and privacy?"*, *"Describe your experience leading a technical team"*, *"How do you handle multi-vendor ETL integrations?"*
